## Units and fidelity limits

The original document is 32 Hz, with `gravity=1`, `walkspeed=1.2`,
`Mariojumpspeed=10`, `Hoverspeed=0.92`, `RocketBlast=25`, `turbospeed=75`,
`Divespeed=35`, `risespeed=0.1`, `TotalWater=10000`, `TotalPower=100`.
See `DOMDocument.xml:11277–11279` and `:11765–11813`.

For new adapters, one original velocity unit is 32 Hz times the chosen 1.375
logical collider scale (44/32 standing height) = 44 px/s. This differs from the
renderer's 1.5x PNG magnification. Original acceleration units scale by 32 squared times 1.375. Repeated
friction/damping uses powers with exponent `32/60`, and per-frame consumption
uses `32/60`. Water is normalized to 0..100: Hover consumes 2.24 units/s, Rocket
5 units per launch, Turbo 1.6 units/s in the ordinary case. Power is 0..100.

The v0.3 walking/jump comfort tuning remains: speeds 280, jumps -430/-510/-610,
gravity 1050 on held ascent and 1600 otherwise, 0.10 s coyote/buffer windows,
and a 0.28 s chain window. These are **not exact original values**. The triple
momentum gate is source-backed; restarting a single jump at low speed is a
stable adapter instead of copying the original no-launch edge case.

Rocket uses a 15/32 s charge and 8/32 s boost. Wall bonk triggers above 11*44 px/s (5*44 while dashing), then pauses for 10/32 s before
rebounding at horizontal 5*44 and vertical -4*44. Nozzle target speed is capped
at 1400 px/s for this static collision lab. Swimming has tuned control/velocity
limits. FLUDD during dives does not yet reproduce the original vector/rotation
coupling. Resource recovery, water detection and charge cancellation are adapters.

## What is complete versus experimental

**Engine foundations implemented and tested:** immutable versioned level data,
viewport-culled tile rendering and queries, static solid/one-way/slope collision,
headroom-safe posture changes, water/material detection, parallax, fixed-step
input latching, controller bindings, pause/restart and resource bounds.

**Functional but experimental movement ports:** low dive/slide, backflip/recovery
roll, material/slope momentum, swim/surface exit, high-speed bonk/rebound, and
all three FLUDD modes. They have source-backed triggers/equations and automated
calculation checks, but have not been compared frame-by-frame to the Flash game
or played in Dolphin/Wii. This is not a claim of complete faithful Story Mode
movement. Manual wall kicks and an unconfirmed side somersault are absent.

**Animation limits:** old standing/walking/jumping/spin/pound frames and mirrors
are retained. New actions have explicit states but use transformed existing
Mario poses: compressed low posture, rotated dive/swim/backflip/roll, airborne
Hover/Rocket and faster walking Turbo. These are clearly temporary animation
adapters, not newly imported authentic clips. The original triple-jump frontflip clip is also not yet reproduced. Exact new action sprite sets,
FLUDD body/jet art and collision rotations remain incomplete. No new original
environment assets were redistributed; tile/background graphics are placeholders.

**Outside this milestone:** Story Mode levels/scripts, moving platforms, arbitrary
Flash/vector slopes or combined ice-slope tiles, power-ups, oxygen/health/drowning, water splashes, nozzle
unlock/pickup systems, enemy interactions, sounds, scenery assets and Level Designer.
The red hazard tile is a lab respawn material, not an implementation of original
lava damage/bouncing. One-way platform drop-through is not implemented.

## Playtest checklist (not yet performed)

Test all three controllers; horizontal/vertical analog dead zones; low-dive
headroom and release; backflip and recovery roll; double-tap dash; triple jump
while moving; wall bonk; slope ascent/descent in both directions; one-way platform
head/foot contacts; water entry, strokes, spin and exit; Hover depletion; Rocket
charge/cancel/boost/recharge; Turbo on ground, airborne and underwater; switching
nozzles; pause during each action; widescreen/PAL pacing and original Wii frame
time. Verify there are no yellow spin squares and existing jump/spin facing stays
correct. Prior user confirmation of old sprite directions does not validate v0.4.
