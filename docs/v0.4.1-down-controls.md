# v0.4.1 Down / crouch / backflip control fix

Reference: Runouw/Super-Mario-63 at `83e81fb6b80d11c4119e420d25298000a6e13f66`,
`Super_Mario_63_2010/LIBRARY/Char/Mario CLIPS/Mario (None) Dive.xml` and
`Super_Mario_63_2010/DOMDocument.xml`.

## Dolphin report being addressed

GameCube controller, v0.4.1 (`6f5be00`): D-pad Down and stick Down "do nothing",
Mario does not crouch, Down + A does not backflip.

## Findings

1. **Input reaches gameplay.** `input_poll` already set `crouch_held` from D-pad
   Down and from stick-down past the threshold, and `game_input_latch` carries it.
   The new host-tested `input_map_gamecube` proves this path.
2. **Crouch state was entered but invisible.** `ACTION_DIVE` started and the
   collider shrank to 26 px, but `player_sprite_select` mapped `PLAYER_CROUCH`
   to atlas frame 0 (standing). The original crouch pose (`Mario Slide (None)`)
   exists only in the optional private `player.art`; without it the HUD shows
   `PRIVATE ART PACK MISSING` and Mario looks unchanged.
3. **Down + A is not the original backflip.** `Dive.xml:136` requires: on the
   ground, |xspeed| < 5, `KeyUP()` held, **and** Right held while `_xscale>0`
   (facing left) or Left held while facing right. Down + Up alone hits
   `Dive.xml:162` (`play()`): the stand-up recovery. The port was correct to
   refuse Down + A; it was wrong in two other places, below.
4. **Recovery window removed.** The original Dive timeline stops at frame 2
   (Slide), `play()` runs frames 3..9, and only frame 9 clears `attack`. The
   onEnterFrame checks keep running during recovery, so away + Up after releasing
   Down still backflips. The port ended the dive instantly on release and let the
   ordinary walk code turn Mario around, so away + A became a normal jump.
5. **Press vs held.** The original tests `KeyUP()` (held) each source frame. The
   port required a fresh press, so "hold A, then push away" did nothing.
6. **Analog diagonal.** A full GameCube down-diagonal (~57,57 raw) scaled to
   0.649, just under the old 0.65 Down threshold, so holding down-away on the stick
   released the crouch.

## Changes

- `source/game/player_special.c`: dive now tracks the source clip frame
  (`dive_frame`, `dive_recovering`) on the 32 Hz source clock. Backflip and roll
  checks use held Jump on every source frame including recovery. Recovery starts
  per `Dive.xml:161-168`; the dive ends at frame 9. Facing stays locked throughout.
  Backflip impulse, 22-frame duration and facing lock are unchanged.
- `PLAYER_DIVE_RECOVER`: frames 0 and 6..9 show `Mario Air (none) - 2` (atlas
  frame 10 / private `ART_RISE`); frames 1..5 show the Slide pose (`PLAYER_CROUCH`).
  Grounded fast slides now also use the Slide pose, matching the bitmap layer.
- `source/game/input_map.c`: pure GameCube mapping, static-asserted against
  libogc constants in `source/platform/input.c`. Analog Down threshold 0.65 → 0.5
  for GameCube, Nunchuk and Classic sticks. D-pad and buttons unchanged.
- `tests/controls_test.c`: 5 regression groups (input mapping, crouch hold and
  recovery, three backflip activation paths in both facings, non-backflip cases,
  jump and repeated airborne spin preserved). Three older tests updated where they
  encoded the instant dive exit.
