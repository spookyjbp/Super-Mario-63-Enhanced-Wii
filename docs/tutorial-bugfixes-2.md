# Tutorial bug repairs, second report (bugs 5, 6, 8, 9, 10, 11)

The three animation bugs (6, 9, 10) had one shared cause: the player art
selector (`source/render/player_art_select.c`) picks exactly one exported body
clip per gameplay state, and three original states had nothing to pick — the
`hurt2` wall bonk and the Main `Sign` pose were never exported or selected, and
the FLUDD spray nested inside the Dive and Frontflip clips (Wait WaterBlast
1157) was exported empty and never drawn. The two graphics bugs (5, 8) also
share a cause: defects left by FFDec, the renderer that turns the SWF into PNGs.

| Bug | Root cause | Fix | Regression test |
|---|---|---|---|
| 5. Lines / seams in the background | FFDec rasterisation, already present in the exported PNGs. (a) FFDec draws a non-smoothed bitmap fill (types 0x42/0x43, all of this pixel art) with texel floor(u - 1/2) where the Flash Player uses floor(u): the art is drawn half a texel off and the first row/column of every fill wraps round to the opposite edge of its tile, giving the white line across the sky (CastleColoredFarBG band 2139) and the thin ground-coloured lines along the top and sides of the bush, waterfall and vine strips (straight or, on sloped pieces, diagonal and dashed). (b) Pieces that share an edge leave see-through cracks or let the art behind leak through (antialiasing conflation). (c) On the Wii, neighbouring 1024 px tiles were placed with `x + width`, so their edges did not match bit for bit. | (a) `tools/swf_bitmap_fix.py` moves every non-smoothed bitmap fill matrix by half a texel in the SWF given to FFDec (28,207 fills), and FFDec renders with supersampling (`useMsaaForExport`). (b) `tools/art_repair.py` fills the remaining cracks (straight or diagonal, < 1.9 px of missing coverage, >= 6 px long) and 1 px seams that cut across differently coloured art; real art lines and wider gaps are kept; transparent texels take their neighbours' colour for bilinear filtering. (c) `course_layer_tile_quad` computes tile edges from absolute image columns. | `tests/test_art_repair.py`: half-texel matrix move, synthetic cracks/seams/real lines, every generated layer free of full-width lines and cracks; `course_art_test`: tile edges equal at any camera offset/scale |
| 6. No wall-bonk animation | No `hurt2` state in the pack or the selector: a bonk drew the fall/walk pose. The bonk itself also ended early. | `hurt2` exported (32 frames) and selected for `ACTION_BONK`; the bonk follows the clip timeline: 10 frames stopped, rebound (yspeed -4, xspeed 5 away from the wall) at frame 10, frame 14 holds until landing, frame 22 holds until a key, ends at 31. Triggered as in CharCode (`abs(xspeed) > 11` into a wall with no action, or dashing above 5); breakable boxes' HitWall also uses it. | `movement_v04_test` (bonk timeline), `art_pack_test` (ART_HURT2 selection, 32 frames) |
| 8. Sign corrupted against the sky | The sign board (shape 982) is a rotated repeating non-smoothed bitmap fill, hit by the same FFDec half-texel offset: a transparent column (alpha 153, 0, 163) inside the board next to its left outline, a light stripe over the sky, plus dark fringes from black transparent texels under bilinear filtering. | Fixed at the source by the half-texel fill correction (the sign needs no repair now); object frames also go through `art_repair`, and object atlases are colour-bled before RGB5A3 encoding. The original artwork is kept. | `test_art_repair.test_sign_board_is_solid` |
| 9. No FLUDD during triple jump / dive | Dive (HDive 1200) and Frontflip (Hfrontflip 1216 > 1214) carry the spray clip 1157 as a nested child; it was rendered empty and nothing drew it. | `tools/export_movement_clips.py` exports clip 1157 frames 1-5 and, per body frame, the nested placement matrices (`assets/generated/player_spray.h`). `user_art.c` draws the spray at the current blast level behind/in front of the body as placed, with the clip's `_yscale` parity, the Dive rotation and the facing mirror. FLUDD keeps working during the moves. | `art_pack_test` (`test_spray_during_specials`) |
| 10. No sign-reading animation | No `Sign` state; opening a sign showed the message at once with Mario in his standing pose. | `Sign` exported and selected while reading. The sign sequence follows the original: 6 frames walking Mario to the sign (`x += (sign.x - x)/5`) with the camera override aimed at the sign, the message, then 6 frames before control returns. One camera move per frame is kept (no shake). | `course_session_test` (sign pose, timeline, camera steady) |
| 11. Loud, distorted coins | `ASND_GetFirstUnusedVoice` returned voices that were idle between music/FLUDD loops, so new sounds round-robined over busy voices; several full-volume coin jingles started on the same tick summed past 16 bits and clipped. | `sfx_pick_voice`: a request for a sound already started less than 2 ticks ago merges into it, at most 2 voices play the same sound (the oldest is replaced), other sounds use free voices 2-15. Every coin is still counted. | `sound_test` (`test_coin_voices`: 10 coins counted, <= 2 jingles) |

Not covered: hurt1/hurt3 knock-back clips, the Rocket nozzle's nested spray
(1349/1370), and the picture-sign pages' original position on the stage.
